Hi Olivia, it’s Mike. I made this page because a message felt too small for what I wanted to say. It isn’t a favour to ask or a reason to reply. It’s only a thank-you, and a few things I’ve been wanting you to know.

What I see in you

In your world, people pick a shade. Here are mine for you. Tap any one.

Determinationtap

I’ve watched you build your beauty brand and keep showing up with your content, even when it takes patience. That kind of consistency is rare. It takes real belief and real work, and it shows.

Kindnesstap

You kept saying hi to me, again and again, even when I didn’t answer. Most people would have stopped. You didn’t, and that tells me exactly what kind of person you are.

Artistrytap

Beauty is your craft, and the care you put into it is clear. You are beautiful, and I mean that as simple admiration, the way you’d say it about someone whose work and presence both shine. What impresses me most is the person behind it all.

Inspirationtap

You inspired me far more than you know. Several things you did made me proud, even from a distance, and I never found the courage to say so. I’m saying it now.

About those hellos

I owe you an honest explanation. Around that time I was in a low place, and my confidence had dropped so far that I didn’t believe I was someone worth talking to, least of all someone like you.

So when you said hi, I didn’t ignore you. I just couldn’t find my voice. I’m sorry it looked like rudeness. It never was. I thought you were wonderful then, and I still do.

I’m in a much better place now, and one thing I wanted to do with that was tell you the truth.

A season that doesn’t go to plan says nothing about who you are.

You’re capable, you’re kind, and you’re doing real things with real heart. Hard seasons come for everyone, and they don’t get a vote on your worth. You keep amazing me.

I really enjoyed our conversations, and I’m grateful for them. I’m also a big fan. I watch, I cheer, and I’ll keep cheering.

With sincere admiration,
Mike
